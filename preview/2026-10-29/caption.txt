Oxygen hemoglobin dissociation curve for USMLE Step 1: why CO poisoning shifts it left

The oxygen dissociation curve shifts right vs left based on P50: right means higher P50, lower affinity, easier unloading. Left is the opposite.
CADET face right: CO2, Acid, 2,3-BPG, Exercise, Temperature. Know this mnemonic cold for Step 1 physiology.
Carbon monoxide poisoning shifts the curve left AND lowers O2 content, while PaO2 is normal and pulse oximetry reads falsely normal.
Anemia lowers O2 content but low Hb alone does not shift the curve. Fetal hemoglobin has higher O2 affinity because it binds 2,3-BPG poorly.
Bohr effect vs Haldane effect: Bohr is H+ and CO2 lowering O2 affinity; Haldane is deoxygenated Hb binding more CO2 and H+.
